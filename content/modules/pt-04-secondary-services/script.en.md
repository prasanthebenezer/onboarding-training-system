# Plant Technology 4: Secondary Services and Safety (script v1, EN)
Source: SMP-MAINT-TRAIN-002 v1, section 5 · Target length: about 2:45 · Status: DRAFT, needs SME review

## Scene 1: Title (0:00)
ON SCREEN: Plant Technology · Module 4 · Secondary Services
NARRATION: Production depends on five secondary services. As maintenance personnel, you will work on all of them, and each has its own safety rules.

## Scene 2: Aspiration (0:10)
ON SCREEN: 1 Aspiration · fans, bag filters, cyclones, rotary airlocks, ductwork, explosion relief vents
NARRATION: One: aspiration. It controls dust across the plant, protecting worker health, preventing dust explosions and maintaining product quality.

## Scene 3: Aspiration safety (0:25)
ON SCREEN: ⚠ LOTO · Never bypass explosion vents · Dust in ducts = fire & explosion
NARRATION: Safety first: always follow lock-out tag-out. Never bypass explosion vents or safety devices. Dust building up in ductwork is a fire and explosion hazard.

## Scene 4: Compressed air (0:42)
ON SCREEN: 2 Compressed Air · compressors, dryers, receivers, filters, regulators, piping
NARRATION: Two: compressed air. It supplies clean, dry air for pneumatic controls, actuators, air-operated equipment and cleaning.

## Scene 5: Electrical and control (0:58)
ON SCREEN: 3 Electrical & PCS · switchgear, MCCs, VFDs, PLCs, HMIs, sensors
NARRATION: Three: electrical installations and the plant control system. They distribute power, and the control system automates and monitors production.

## Scene 6: Electrical safety (1:12)
ON SCREEN: ⚠ Qualified personnel only · LOTO · Verify zero energy · Arc flash risk
NARRATION: Electrical work is for qualified personnel only. Follow lock-out tag-out and verify zero energy state before you start. Arc flash hazards exist at electrical panels.

## Scene 7: Liquids (1:30)
ON SCREEN: 4 Liquid Installations · tanks, heating, pumps, flow meters, liquid scales, spray nozzles
NARRATION: Four: liquid installations. They store, heat and add liquid ingredients like molasses, fats and oils.

## Scene 8: Steam (1:45)
ON SCREEN: 5 Steam Supply · boilers, feed water, PRVs, steam traps, condensate return, safety valves
NARRATION: Five: steam supply. It provides steam for conditioning, pelleting, extrusion and heating liquids.

## Scene 9: Steam safety (2:00)
ON SCREEN: ⚠ High temperature & pressure · Never work on pressurized steam systems
NARRATION: Steam systems run at high temperature and pressure. Burns and scalds are serious hazards. Never work on a pressurized steam system.

## Scene 10: Recap (2:15)
ON SCREEN: Aspiration · Compressed Air · Electrical & PCS · Liquids · Steam
NARRATION: Five services: aspiration, compressed air, electrical and control, liquids, and steam. Know them, and work on them safely. Now take the quiz.
