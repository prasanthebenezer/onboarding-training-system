# Plant Technology 2: Pre-Grinding vs Post-Grinding (script v1, EN)

## Scene 1: Title (0:00)
ON SCREEN: Plant Technology · Module 2 · Plant Configurations
NARRATION: A feed mill can be built in two main ways: pre-grinding or post-grinding. The difference is simply where grinding sits in the process. Let's look at both.

## Scene 2: Pre-grinding flow (0:12)
ON SCREEN: Intake/Cleaning → Size Reduction → Dosing/Weighing → Mixing → Pelleting/Cooling → Finished Products
NARRATION: In a pre-grinding plant, raw materials are ground before dosing and weighing. The sequence is: intake and cleaning, size reduction, dosing and weighing, mixing, pelleting and cooling, and finished products.

## Scene 3: Pre-grinding advantages (0:38)
ON SCREEN: ✓ Uniform particle size ✓ Consistent grind spec ✓ Simpler grinder operation ✓ Less cross-contamination
NARRATION: Pre-grinding gives uniform particle size across all batches, and consistent grinding specifications whatever the recipe. The grinder runs on dedicated material streams, which makes operation simpler, and it reduces the risk of contamination between different raw materials.

## Scene 4: Post-grinding flow (1:05)
ON SCREEN: Intake/Cleaning → Dosing/Weighing → Size Reduction → Mixing → Pelleting/Cooling → Finished Products
NARRATION: In a post-grinding plant, the order changes. Raw materials are dosed and weighed first, then ground together. The sequence is: intake and cleaning, dosing and weighing, size reduction, mixing, pelleting and cooling, and finished products.

## Scene 5: Post-grinding advantages (1:30)
ON SCREEN: ✓ Recipe-specific particle size ✓ Grind only what is needed ✓ Adjust per batch ✓ Better product control
NARRATION: Post-grinding allows particle size to be optimized for each recipe. It is energy efficient because only the required quantity is ground. Grind specifications can be adjusted per batch, giving better control over the final product.

## Scene 6: Side by side (1:55)
ON SCREEN: Both flows stacked; the "Size Reduction" step highlighted moving position
NARRATION: Notice that both plants start with intake and cleaning, and both finish with mixing, pelleting and cooling, and finished products. Only the position of grinding changes, before or after dosing.

## Scene 7: Recap (2:12)
ON SCREEN: PRE = grind → dose · POST = dose → grind
NARRATION: Remember: pre-grinding means grind, then dose. Post-grinding means dose, then grind. Now take the quiz to complete this module.
